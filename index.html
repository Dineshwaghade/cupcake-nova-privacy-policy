Cupcake Nova 2048

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Cupcake Nova 2048 has no accounts and no sign-in, and it never asks who you are. Your scores and your game in progress are stored on your own phone and are not sent anywhere. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's advertising service collects in order to show ads, and you are asked about that separately.

What the game stores on your phone

  • Your best score, the biggest cupcake you have baked, and how many games you have played.
  • The game in progress, so you can carry on where you stopped.
  • How many undos you have left.
  • Your sound, music, vibration and display settings.

All of this is kept in the app's own private storage on your device. It is not uploaded, not shared, and never seen by us. Uninstalling the game deletes it. You can also erase it at any time from Settings → Reset progress inside the game.

Advertising

Cupcake Nova 2048 is free and is paid for by ads served by Google AdMob. There are three kinds, and this is all of them:

Banner
A strip along the bottom edge of the screen, below the menu and below the board. It never covers the game.
Full screen
Only on a tap that leaves a game behind: after a game has ended and you tap to play again or go home, or when you choose to start a new game part-way through one. Never in the middle of play, and never when the app opens.
Rewarded video
Only if you choose to tap a button offering extra undos or a chance to keep baking. It always says what it gives you before you tap it, and it never plays on its own.

To show those ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier, device and app information, and approximate location derived from your IP address. Google uses this to serve and measure ads, and to detect fraud. How Google handles that information is described here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

We do not receive any of that information ourselves, and we do not sell personal information.

Your choices about ads

  • If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown the first time the game runs. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy options inside the game.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID from your phone's own settings, usually under Settings → Google → Ads.
  • You never have to watch a rewarded video. The game can always be played and restarted without one.

Permissions

The game itself asks for nothing. Every permission it declares comes from the Google Mobile Ads library it uses:

INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
Fetching ads.
AD_ID
The advertising identifier described above.
ACCESS_ADSERVICES_*
Android's Privacy Sandbox, declared by the ads library.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, WAKE_LOCK
Declared by a library the ads SDK depends on. The game does not use them.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for an account, an email address, or any personal details.
  • It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • It has no chat, no user-generated content, and no social features. The Share button only opens your phone's own share sheet with a link to the game.
  • The game is built into the app, so it plays with no connection at all.

Children

Cupcake Nova 2048 is not directed to children under 13, and is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families programme. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through this app, please contact us at the address below and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date at the top, and ships with the next update of the game. The game shows the same policy inside it, so it can be read offline; the date at the top tells you which version you are reading.
